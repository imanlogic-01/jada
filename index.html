JADA.

Singer, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist and creative entrepreneur from Hertfordshire, England.

Rooted in powerful vocals, musicianship and a deep love for live performance, JADA creates music and experiences centred around storytelling, emotional connection and creative expression. Her musical journey began in church and has evolved into a multidisciplinary practice combining live music, movement, visual storytelling and audience connection.
